Business

NVIDIA is a full-stack computing company. The Company's data center platform combines
GPUs, networking, and software, and is used for accelerated computing and generative AI
workloads. The Compute & Networking segment includes the Company's Data Center accelerated
computing platforms, networking, and automotive platforms.

Risk Factors

The Company's results depend on demand for its data center products, which can fluctuate.
Long product lead times and reliance on third-party foundries and contract manufacturers
expose the Company to supply constraints. A significant portion of revenue is concentrated
among a limited number of customers, and the loss of one or more major customers could harm
operating results. Export controls and trade restrictions affecting sales of advanced chips
to certain regions could adversely affect revenue.

Results of Operations

Data Center revenue grew substantially, driven by demand for accelerated computing and
generative AI. Gross margin expanded as data center products represented a larger share of
revenue. The Company expects continued investment in research and development.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

The Company believes its existing cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities, together
with cash generated from operations, will be sufficient to meet its operating requirements.
